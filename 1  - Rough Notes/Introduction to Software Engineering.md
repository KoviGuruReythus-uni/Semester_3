Are developers even needed anymore? 
	Developers in the traditional sense might not even be needed, but software engineering is not just coding. 
	The _engineering_ part of SWE is becoming more important than the pure coding part.
___
## Definitions to get an idea of what these things mean
Software:
	Computer programs, procedures, and possibly associated documentation and data pertaining to the operation of a computer system 

Software System:
	A system made up of software, hardware, and data that provides it primary value by the execution of the software

System:
	Combination of interacting elements organised to achieve one or more stated purposes.

Engineering:
	The application of a systematic, disciplined, quantifiable approach to structures, machines, products, systems, or processes.
___

## Traditional engineering workflow 
1. Customer requirements, Standards, Existing components
2. Iterations and trade off
3. Simulation, analysis 
4. prototyping and testing
5. production
